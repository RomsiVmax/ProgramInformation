The rendering engine

In the past we used tkinter, pythons standard rendering engine. It was extremely laggy and old. We relied on literal bugs - yes, you heard it right - BUGS for ICreator to work.

What we have done about it

We decided our best choice would be to begin rebuilding the entire program in another rendering engine. As we knew that it will take us quite some time to do that, we chose wisely, and came to the conclusion that the best rendering engine we could mirgrate our program to would be DearPyGui.
